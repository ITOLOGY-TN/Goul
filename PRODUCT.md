# Goul

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Native macOS 26, Swift 6.2 and SwiftUI/AppKit.

## Users and purpose
Ahmed uses Goul personally to hold a key, dictate, and insert text into another application. English and French, detected automatically per utterance; no language setting.

## Constraints
Free local speech inference; no cloud dictation or account. Initial model downloads may require internet. Personal installation on an M3 MacBook Air with 16 GB RAM.

## Brand commitments
App name: Goul. User requested One Piece, Straw Hat and pirate-crew imagery and delegated artwork choices.

## Privacy
No audio recording files. Session transcripts remain in memory by default; persistent history is opt-in.
